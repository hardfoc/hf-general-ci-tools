{#- Mermaid on demand. Material loads Mermaid (about 950 KB of script, a few hundred ms of main-thread work on a phone) on every page with a diagram, as soon as the page loads, even when the diagram is far down the page. Material skips its own loader when a global `mermaid` exists and then only calls `mermaid.initialize(config)` once and `mermaid.render(id, text)` per diagram. This stand-in answers those two calls and loads the real Mermaid only when a diagram comes near the screen (or the page is printed); Material renders it as before, with the theme's colours. Without IntersectionObserver every diagram renders at once, as before. tests/test_plugin.py checks that Material's bundle still uses these two calls. -#}
